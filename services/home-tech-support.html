Home & Personal Tech Support in Levy County

Fast, honest help with the tech problems that happen at home — from computers that won't turn on to Wi-Fi that won't reach the back bedroom.

Not every tech problem is a business problem. Sometimes it's just a computer that won't turn on, a Wi-Fi dead spot in the back bedroom, or photos you're terrified of losing. We help with that too — same local, straight-answer approach we bring to business clients.

No mysteries, no jargon you have to pretend to understand, and no selling you things you don't need. Just the fix, explained clearly, at a fair price.

What We Help With

  • Computer repair & troubleshooting — Computer won't turn on, running slow, frozen, blue screen errors, or just acting strange. We diagnose the actual problem and give you honest options: repair, upgrade, or replace. Read: What to try when your computer won't turn on
  • Virus & malware removal — Pop-ups, fake warnings, browser hijacking, or ransomware threats. We clean it up and show you how to avoid it next time. Read: How to know if your computer has a virus
  • New computer/device setup — Bought a new laptop or desktop and don't want to lose your files, passwords, or settings? We handle the transfer and setup so everything just works when you sit down.
  • Home Wi-Fi setup & dead-spot troubleshooting — Router setup, mesh system installation, fixing dead spots, or just figuring out why the Wi-Fi keeps dropping. Built for Florida construction (block walls, metal roofs, long distances). Read: Wi-Fi extender vs. mesh system
  • Data recovery & personal file/photo backup — Hard drive failed, accidentally deleted files, or just want your photos backed up properly so they don't disappear. We recover what's recoverable and set up backups that actually run automatically.
  • Smart home & camera system setup — Thermostats, security cameras, doorbell cams, smart locks — getting them installed, connected, and working together without mystery app logins you'll forget.

How It Works

Most home tech support jobs fall into one of two categories:

Remote fixes — For software issues, slow computers, virus removal, and basic troubleshooting, we can often connect remotely and fix it from our desk. Faster for you, cheaper for you, and you don't have to unhook anything.

On-site visits — For hardware problems, Wi-Fi setup, new equipment installation, or anything that requires being in front of the actual device. We serve homes throughout Levy County — Chiefland, Bronson, Williston, Cedar Key, and surrounding areas.

What This Costs

Home tech support is typically billed hourly or as a flat rate per job, depending on what's needed. Simple remote fixes (virus removal, slow computer cleanup) are usually flat-rate. Bigger jobs (data recovery, full network setup) are quoted after we understand what's involved. We'll give you an honest estimate before we start — no surprise bills.

Not Sure What's Wrong? That's fine — most people aren't. Just tell us what it's doing (or not doing), and we'll figure out the actual problem. "My computer is slow" or "the Wi-Fi doesn't work in the bedroom" is enough to start.
